How values work

The list does not use the site's raw point value. It uses position. The card at rank 25 is the unit: 1.00. Every other card is placed against it on a curve.

The curve is non-linear. Going from rank 25 to rank 1 is not a straight line. It is a steep climb, because trade demand at the top of the list does not scale evenly. Editors can reshape the curve by adding anchor points (for example, rank 1, rank 10, rank 50) and the site fits a curve between them.

  • Value is what a card is worth in rank-25 units.
  • Holo value follows the community sheet where known, otherwise 3x the normal value.
  • Demand is how many people want the card. It is a separate score and never changes the value on its own.
  • N/A means a card is new and has not been checked by an editor yet.

Demand vs value

A card can be valuable and still be easy to trade, or cheap and impossible to find. Value is the price. Demand is the appetite. This list keeps them apart on purpose. Normal and holo copies have their own demand score.

Demand is scored from D to S. A newly added card starts at no score until a moderator sets one.
